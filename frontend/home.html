Our Identity

Why மருதம்?

In Sangam literature, மருதம் (Marutham) is one of the five classical Tamil landscape categories — the fertile cropland tinai, defined by paddy fields, rivers, and the Marutham tree with its bright pink flowers.

We chose this name because we carry that same spirit: the richness of the land, the freshness of the harvest, and the connection between farmers and families. The bloom pink you see is taken from the actual மருதம் flower — a reminder that every meal begins in a field.

Our Story

Rooted in the Land,
Built for the People

Marutham Agrolink was born from a simple observation: Tamil Nadu has some of India's most productive farmland, yet farmers earn the least because of long, opaque supply chains. Families pay inflated prices for produce that travelled hundreds of kilometres.

We built a direct marketplace that connects the farmer in Pudukkottai to the family in the next village — with transparent pricing, morning delivery, and digital tools that farmers can actually use on a feature phone.
